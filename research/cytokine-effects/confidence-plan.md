# Confidence and candidate coverage (post-hoc audit)

This audit follows inspection of 90-way results; it is not a prospective registration. First separate candidate difficulty from gene coverage on Xue: use the **exact same previously computed 90-way scores and genes**, but restrict the candidate names to the four known ligands. Report true ranks and score gaps in both settings.

A potential user-facing output is 'uncertain' rather than a compulsory cytokine call. Evaluate the fixed own-type score only. Each query is a study-level donor mean for one ligand and lineage, not a single cell; do not train on individual donors. Use 24 h only for Yuan (6 h repeats the same study). Primary confidence measure: top1 minus runner-up Spearman correlation; no fitting of molecular profiles. Select a threshold using other studies only, with each study equally weighted and each task equally weighted within study. Among thresholds at unique training gaps, maximize accepted proportion subject to accuracy ≥80% among accepted (ratio of weighted correct to accepted), ≥3 training studies and ≥2 true ligand labels represented among accepted. If infeasible, abstain on all test cases. Apply once per held-out study. Also evaluate one threshold fit on the five current studies then locked for external NK array contrasts (if the GEO raw matrices permit comparable identification). Summarize incorrect calls, coverage and accuracy versus no abstention; avoid declaring safety from perfect precision on very low coverage. Secondary artificial missing-class stress: remove the true reference *only at evaluation*, take top remaining and second remaining as the new gap and count false accepts. This is not a naturally collected open-set cohort. A rule should not be recommended if it accepts a substantial fraction of missing-class examples or cannot cover multiple studies. No post-hoc adjustment of the target 80% threshold. New use of archived studies whose derived C7 labels were previously evaluated is not a new experimental cohort for the project.
