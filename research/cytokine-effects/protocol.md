# Frozen before reading atlas effect scores

**Question:** Does borrowing across cell types improve prediction of the *sizes* of responses, and can it improve identification of cell-type-specific response rather than shared activation? This is a new endpoint on previously inspected Wood/Parse cohorts, not a new independent cohort. Use the Allen uncensored DESeq2 gene-level output (not top-500 membership), Wood raw pseudobulk mean log2 fold changes, the four previously fixed lineages (mono→CD14, naive B, naive CD4, NK→CD56dim), and IL-2/IL-4. Restrict to common finite symbols across all four lineages in a ligand; do not turn missing or untested genes into zero. Keep only source genes ≥1 CPM in at least six Wood pseudobulks over both arms and four lineages. Report eligible counts and ascertainment; if Allen contains only a filtered list, call results conditional on that list. Independent target DESeq2 coefficients are noisy estimates, not true effects.

**Methods:** use own type, other three only, half own plus half other, average of all four, predict no response (zero). Introduce a source-only cross-validated linear weight minimizing squared error on held-out Wood donor responses, clipped to [0,1]. Within each ligand and training subset fit one common weight over all lineages and eligible source genes, with leave-one-donor-out predictions from the remaining training donors. Never use evaluation gene membership or target coefficients to estimate the weight. At one donor use the fixed half blend and label the fallback. No use of target values to fit scales or choose windows. Use all balanced subsets of 1/2/4/6 donors; report six-donor results as primary. Comparison gate for the adaptive weight: lower macro effect RMSE than own, half and the all-lineage mean on **both** cytokines. Define RMSE per lineage before macro averaging. Report rank correlation, and centered RMSE after subtracting the mean across the four types for each gene, on the same symbols. For estimating across-cell specificity also score four-way maximum-response type accuracy, splitting credit among predictions tied at the maximum and omitting exactly tied target maxima. Chance among four types is 25%; report sensitivity to permutation of all 24 target lineage mappings, with cautions about exchangeability. Repeated donor subsets are not replications. No post-hoc selection of a new best model.

**Interpretation control:** for m source types and source own response x_i, let A be the mean of the m types. Fixed-weight estimates satisfy f_i = A + ((mw−1)/(m−1))(x_i−A). Thus a uniform weight above 1/m preserves all within-gene type orderings. Confirm empirically that the half blend and own type make identical type choices (within numeric tolerance); a smaller centered RMSE from shrinkage does not demonstrate improved cell identification. Equal averaging has no type-discrimination ability. Report null/shuffled correspondence separately from prediction error. No clinical-use inference.

**Heterogeneous alternative frozen before scoring:** estimate an additional four weights (one per lineage) using the same within-source leave-donor-out squared error; no target fitting. This can change lineage ordering unlike the common-weight comparator. To prioritize a method claim, require lower overall AND centered RMSE, and higher maximum-type accuracy than own and half on each of IL-2 and IL-4 at six donors. Also run a gene- and lineage-matched control for cytokine specificity: within each source ligand use the other target cytokine's results on the same common genes. This holds gene and lineage identities fixed, not expression magnitude or ligand equivalence. Archived aggregate target coefficients do not permit target-donor uncertainty intervals.

**Structured comparator:** with ≥2 source donors, cross-fit a nonnegative mixture of all source lineages for each query lineage. Use the same leave-one-donor-out training predictions and squared-error objective as the scalar weight, but constrain weights to sum to one. The other lineages need not contribute equally. Fit the small constrained least-squares problem on source data alone; no target-calibrated scale, target genes, cell labels or target-derived hyperparameters. At one donor use the same fixed-half fallback. Test both within-ligand and transfer of the six-donor Wood weight matrix from IL-2 to IL-4 and vice versa. Same advancement gate as for heterogeneous weights. Exploratory comparison across models, not a claim to have invented convex regression.

**Functional transfer endpoint:** before examining pathway results, use all 50 MSigDB Hallmark v2025.1.Hs gene sets with ≥15 shared genes per panel. Within each lineage, convert each gene's effect to a centered percentile rank. Score a pathway by its mean gene rank (descriptive gene-set activity, not a receptor or protein assay). Correlate the pathway vectors of source and target within each lineage and average across lineages. Also evaluate after subtracting the cross-lineage mean pathway score per pathway. Repeat for the wrong cytokine on the same universe. No selecting favorable pathways after viewing scores; inference remains conditional on two human cytokines.
