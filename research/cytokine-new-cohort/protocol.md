# Frozen design: independent human IL-4 target

Written after inspecting study and file metadata, before computing any target effects or evaluating transfer. This is a local analysis plan, not external preregistration.

- **New held-out target:** Yuan et al., [GSE252736](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE252736), the RNA-seq subseries of GSE252738. Use all published purified human CD14+ monocyte RNA-seq pairs for IL-4 versus time-matched untreated control: three biological replicate pairs at 24 h (primary, matched to Parse's time) and two pairs at 6 h (secondary timing check, not an independent study). Match arms by deposited time and replicate suffix, corroborated by descriptions and figure legends; do not match across times. Exclude the TNF and IL-4+TNF arms, ATAC/ChIP, and mouse cohort. Both target arms share overnight M-CSF conditioning, so this is IL-4 addition on a matched background, not fresh PBMC exposure. No prior target labels or C7 lists are used.
- **Target effects:** Gene counts from the deposited matrix. Compute library totals over all rows, retain only nonempty unique gene names and genes with at least 1 CPM in at least half the libraries of a time contrast; actual pairing for effect calculation uses log2((count + 0.5)/(library size + 1) × 10⁶), then treatment minus control. Mean across the paired differences gives an equally weighted effect. Use exact case-sensitive symbol matching, no imputation or alias matching. For 24 h, recompute the target mean after omitting each replicate, keeping the primary assay universe fixed. Output no sample-level data to the repository.
- **Source rankings fixed independently of Yuan:** Existing paired Wood PBMC log2-CPM effects (all six donors, 48 h) use the prior moderated-analysis *expression filter only*: ≥1 CPM in at least six donor × condition × lineage pseudobulks. Parse/Allen uses `log2FoldChange` in the uncensored DESeq2 release, four comparable lineages, with only genes having a finite effect in all four. Across source studies use one representative per lineage: mono / CD14 monocyte, B_n / Naive B cell, CD4_Tn / Naive CD4 T cell, NK / CD56dim NK cell. Own = monocyte, other = equally weighted mean of the other three, half = fixed arithmetic mean of own and other. No target calibration. IL-2 source rankings vs the IL-4 target serve as a specificity comparator, not as a second IL-2 target.
- **Primary endpoint:** P@100 against the top 500 target effects per direction (strictly positive/negative), computed separately for induced and repressed genes. Both assays rank within the intersection of the target detection filter and the respective source assay filter; every method has the same gene and shortlist budget. Tie-break shortlist cutoffs by gene symbol. Compare half minus own, with direction mean giving equal weight to induced and repressed results. Provide both sources separately; no pooling as if they were independent targets.
- **Secondary endpoints:** directional average precision for recovery of the same target top 500 across the full ranked shared universe (ties evaluated together; random prevalence = 500/N when all 500 exist); sign agreement and signed mean target LFC among the 100 source predictions; global Spearman rho (average ties). Under whole-assay scoring, construct target lists without considering source availability, and use source-only ranks at fixed budget to show the effect of platform/availability. Leave-one-replicate-out ranges describe target sensitivity, not statistical intervals. Report source ranks and target contrasts as tasks, not independent replications; no tuned threshold, p-value, or winner selection across endpoints.
