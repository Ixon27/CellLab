# Xue: blinded ligand retrieval in a second new human cohort

Locked after checking metadata only, before target effects or matched ligand scores. Use [Xue et al., GSE46903](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE46903), a human monocyte-derived macrophage array study not used in the earlier manuscript. Restrict to GM-CSF-differentiated macrophages at 72 h with a time-matched untreated `con` and *each* of four single-cytokine treatments IL4, IL13, IL10, IFNg in the same published donor: BC58, BC76, BC78. This forms a balanced 3 donors × 5 arms panel. Do not use any other stimulation, other culture times, undifferentiated subsets, or mixed/unknown treatments. Replicate arrays in an arm (if any) are averaged within donor. Arrays are already log2 quantile-normalized. Use nonempty unambiguous gene symbols from GPL6947, median across probes, then subtract each donor's matched control. Differentiate array effects from uncertainty estimates; every donor shares one baseline across ligands.

Source = published 24 h Parse gene-level DESeq2 LFC for the 4 target ligands. Model estimates: matched CD14 monocyte, equally weighted mean of naïve B/naïve CD4/CD56dim NK, fixed half these two. Freeze the identical gene universe (finite all four source lineages, all four ligands, explicit target probe mapping). Group analysis: target mean over the 3 donors; also score each donor separately and all three leave-one-donor-out two-donor means. No target-fitted gene selection or weights.

Primary question: given a blinded gene response to one of the four ligands, can we identify *which* using the source responses? 4×4 matrix of gene-level Spearman correlations between each experimental target row and each candidate ligand. Predictions made independently per row (no forcing one match per ligand); chance for top-1 is 1/4, fractional credit for ties. Evaluate diagonal macro correlation, incorrect-candidate mean, gap, top1, MRR, and labels under all 24 permutations of the target-stimulus correspondence. Descriptive exact tail includes observed permutation; units are four selected ligands, not genes or 12 independent cohorts. IL-4 and IL-13 are retained as deliberately hard near neighbors, not merged. Report per-ligand, per-donor, all incorrect matches and coverage. A priori evidence for panel-level discrimination: own-lineage model must recover all four in the group means and >chance in each donor. As a secondary check report RMSE and directional P@100 vs target top 500 on the common universe. These are different assay contexts/times (mixed PBMC 24 h vs differentiated macrophages 72 h), and this is not clinical diagnosis nor a claim about all cytokines or all macrophages.
