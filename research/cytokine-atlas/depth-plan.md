# Wood equal-cell-budget falsification check

Plan before scoring Wood donor data. Does the advantage of pooling at k=1 persist when donors change but total cells do not? Use exactly the Wood donor IDs, annotations, lineage–Parse mapping and stimuli of the independent source test. Keep the primary included lineages (≥10 cells in every donor/condition). For each lineage use total B=6×floor(min donor-condition cells/6) in every treatment and baseline. This uses all lineages for which k=6 is feasible; budgets can differ across lineages, which is reported. Compare k=1,2,3,6 donors, selecting exactly B/k cells without replacement from each donor/condition. Common random donor subsets for both stimuli, each k cycles all subsets in shuffled order, 60 draws per k (k=1 has 6, k=2 has 15, k=3 has 20, k=6 has 1 subset). Randomize cells in each draw; use the same baseline sample for the two cytokines. Keep the *same* per-donor log-CPM ratio and equal-weight mean across donors at every depth, not sum-then-log across donors. Compare own, other and fixed half blend on directional gene retrieval using identical draws. Repeated computations on six people are not independent replication; do not construct p-values from repetitions. This is a low-depth test, not an estimate of the causal benefit of recruiting new patients. All-cell and downsampled estimates are separate.
