# External human targets, frozen before looking at overlaps

Source: human Parse/Allen (same signatures now used for prediction, not truth). Targets: ImmunoSigDB v2025.1.Hs independently curated human microarray contrasts. Purposive holdout, **not** an exhaustive systematic review. Four accessions confirmed as Homo sapiens via EBI BioStudies. GSE22886 NK IL-2 and IL-15; GSE12198 NK low/high IL-2 (7-day culture vs primary cells, confounded by culture); GSE2770 activated CD4 T cells IL-4 and IL-12 at 2/6/48 h; GSE20198 activated CD4 IL-12. Activation-only controls in these T cell experiments are not resting PBMC controls. GSE2770 and GSE20198 both involve early Th differentiation; retain accession-level results and do not assume donor/laboratory independence merely from accession. Exclude mouse accessions, compound stimuli and unmapped cell types. Fixed 11 contrasts; no choosing best time/dose. Up in control-vs-stimulus means DOWN after stimulus. No results from these targets used to choose pool weights.

For each ligand give each gene a signed membership score: +1 in Up, -1 in Down, zero if unlisted, average within each of the five mapped source lineages. Compare own, mean of four other lineages, equal blend. Top 100 direction-positive genes; exact expected number of hits under uniform ties, unfilled slots count as misses. Primary outcome: expected hits / 100 averaged first over contrasts/directions within accession and then across accessions. Report each accession, up/down, and a specificity control: for identical targets compare to predictions from the other three test ligands (IL-2, IL-4, IL-12, IL-15); related cytokines are deliberately difficult controls. No treating ranked genes as biological replicates. This is a new-target stress test of borrowing, not a donor-count test.
