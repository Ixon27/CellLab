## Protein Extraction

1. Detach cells with trypsin and centrifuge in 1300 rpm for 3 min.
2. Remove supernatant and add 100ul of RIPA buffer (RIPA buffer- 1X with 1X phosphatase inhibitor and 1X protease inhibitor). Put in ice immediately
3. Crush cells with pipetting and centrifuge it at 13000rpm for 20 min at 4°C, collect supernatant.
4. Dilute the sample 10 times with RIPA buffer (4ul sample+36ul RIPA buffer).
5. Prepare for BCA assay.

## BCA assay

1. Mix BCA A with BCA B in 50:1 and then add 10ul of sample, total volume of solution required is 200ul. {Std- 8\*3=24\*200=4800ul//N sample-(N+1)\*3\*200}.
2. Prepare standards for determining the concentration of protein sample.
3. Calculation: take reading from plate reader instrument at 562nm with incubation for 15 min, average it and calculate concentration with the help of equation in graph obtained, retake the reading.
4. Incubate the sample for further use.

Plate reader 순서: New → Set temperature-37°C → Delay-30min → Read → Ok → Absorbance-562nm → Full plate → Select column → open lid → Ok ok ok

## PAGE sample preparation

1. Each well 15ul.
2. Protein concentration- 20ug.
3. Loading buffer 5X so make it 1X, therefore for 15ul we need only 3ul.
4. RIPA- 15ul-(loading buffer+ Protein concentration).
5. Warm sample at 105° for 7 min and spin it and store at -80° if experiment scheduled for next day.
6. Add ladder (3ul) against each sample, also each sample should have control.

## PAGE preparation

1. Prepare resolving gel (RG) 8ml-1.5mm thick plate, 5ml for 0.75mm plate. \*note- add linkers at last.
2. Set the stacking plate and then check for any leaking with the help of isopropanol.
3. Then pour RG with added linkers till lower green bar.
4. Add isopropanol to remove bubbles above the RG let it solidify.
5. Prepare stacking gel, remove isopropanol and add 2ml or unto the brim of the apparatus and put comb immediately avoiding bubbles.
6. Let everything solidify and add sample.

## PAGE running

1. Use used 1X SDS PAGE (running) buffer outside dam and new 1X SDS PAGE buffer inside.
2. Run PAGE in 100 V for 1 hour or till the band reaches the end of the gel.

## Nitrocellulose transfer

1. Prepare transferring buffer 10X Tris-glycine buffer+ methanol+ water using specified bottle. Put in freezer and use later.
2. Dip black foam, white pad and tray in used transfer buffer.
3. At black side of tray → foam → white pad → gel → cellulose membrane → pad → foam → close the tray. All this should be done in used transfer buffer.
4. Put black side at black side of apparatus, electron moves from black to red side, remove bubble between membrane and gel.
5. Transfer the loaded tray in the apparatus with the empty tray.
6. Pour the new buffer in the apparatus and surround it with ice.
7. Connect it with electric field of 100V for 90 mins.
8. Add red dye to check the bands which can be later removed with the 1X TBST washing.

## Western Blot detection

1. Block the site with 5% skimmed milk for 1 hour.
2. Wash with 1X TBST for 5 min 3 times each.
3. Take transparent film and stick it from 3 sides and then put the Nitro Cellulose membrane (NC) strips inside with the help of forceps.
4. Add primary antibody 1:250, 1ul in 250ul 5% BSA.
5. Incubate overnight at 4 degrees.
6. Next day incubate at RT for 1 hour and then wash (3 times- 10 min) with TBST and block with skimmed milk for 1 hr.
7. Washing 3 times. *[메모] 1× TBST (평행상자 (판독 불확실)) : 5min × 3, Slowly shake.*
8. Add 1:2500 secondary antibodies (in skimmed milk) as per the species for 1 hr. *[메모] → 5min×3 with TBST(1x) 세척. / 2ml skim milk + 0.8ul antibody*
9. Protein detection with WB instrument *[메모] 사진 찍기 전 fresh TBST 올리기 (판독 불확실)*
10. Prepare dilution dye 1:1 of ECL select or ECL prime (total 300ul for each strip). *[메모] → 1X TBST 세척, 5분 ×1*
11. Put the blot in WB film and under the WB instrument select blot-> colorimeter-> position blot-. Chemiluminescence (high sensi or high resolution)-> select time frame-> run.
12. Capture photos and merge them.
13. Remove the antibodies with stripping buffer for 15 min.
14. Then follow steps 1 to 12 for β-actin (1:5000).
